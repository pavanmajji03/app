What we’re trying to build (the real goal)

We want to build a platform where fans can invest in their favorite content creators and earn returns linked to the creator’s performance.

Creators get upfront funding.
Fans get performance-linked payouts.

Because real investing is regulated and expensive to launch immediately, we do it in two steps:

Simulation MVP (no real money) to prove demand + build the AI underwriting engine

Real-money launch in the US via a regulated partner (funding portal)

Phase 1: MVP = Channel-Level Simulation Marketplace (No Real Money)
Why channel-level first

For the MVP we start with channel-level campaigns (not video-level) because it’s:

easier to measure and track overall performance

simpler forecasting (fewer edge cases)

faster to launch and prove traction

✅ Later, once we have traction and more data, we expand to:

channel campaigns (ongoing) AND/OR

video-level campaigns (specific upcoming videos) depending on what the creator lists

MVP flow (simple step-by-step)
1) Creator onboarding (super simple, no OAuth needed)

Creator only enters:

YouTube channel URL or @handle

Optional (to improve forecasting):

Instagram / TikTok / X links

upcoming video details (title, description, format, planned date)

2) We analyze “everything we can” (public + creator-provided signals)

We automatically pull and analyze as many signals as possible to build a strong underwriting report.

YouTube (public data)

subscribers, total views, upload frequency

last ~30 videos + their views/likes/comments

how fast recent videos grow (view velocity)

consistency vs hit-or-miss (volatility)

dependence on viral videos (concentration risk)

Social signals (public)

follower growth

engagement rate

posting consistency

momentum (is the creator rising or falling across platforms?)

Attention / trend / news signals (public)

spikes in mentions/attention

whether topics are trending

notable public events (collabs, announcements, controversies) if detectable

if upcoming video topic looks “hot” based on trend signals

Upcoming video analysis (optional)
If the creator shares next video title/description, the AI predicts likely performance for that specific video by comparing:

creator’s past content performance

similar creators/topics

current trend/attention signals

(In short: we analyze every public signal + every creator-provided detail we can.)

3) AI Underwriting Report (the “wow output”)

For each channel (and optionally a planned video), we generate:

Forecast ranges (3 scenarios)

Low / Base / High forecast for views for next 30/90/180 days

Low / Base / High estimated revenue range (proxy), derived from views × RPM assumptions

What Low/Base/High means (simple)

Low = conservative floor (designed to be exceeded most of the time)

Base = typical expectation

High = upside scenario

Risk breakdown

stability/volatility

growth trend

concentration risk (depends on 1–2 hits or not)

cadence reliability

confidence score + plain-English “why” explanation

4) Creator creates a campaign page (simulation)

MVP campaigns are channel-level.

Creator chooses:

term: 6 / 12 / 24 months

revenue share % (example: 5%)

This generates a public page fans can share.

5) Fans browse creators + payout calculator

Fans browse like a marketplace and open a campaign page.

They can use a simple calculator:
“If I put $100, what might I earn?”

We show a payout range:

Low / Base / High projected payouts over the chosen term
(based on campaign terms + the forecast)

6) Paper investing + paper payouts (simulation that feels real)

Fans allocate fake money into campaigns (paper portfolio).

Every month, the system produces:

paper payout statements

based on the creator’s actual performance updates (public data tracked over time)

So the product experience feels real:
invest → track performance → receive statements → see payouts
but still no real money is collected.

7) Traction proof (what we measure)

Fans submit a non-binding IOI:

“If real investing is live, I’d invest $X”

We track:

creators onboarded

campaigns created

fans signed up

IOIs and average ticket size

repeat usage/retention

forecast accuracy over time

This becomes proof for raising funding.

Phase 2: Real-money launch (US only at first)

Once the MVP shows traction, we launch real investing in the US via a regulated funding portal (Reg CF path).

The portal handles: legal offering, KYC/AML, escrow, transaction flow
